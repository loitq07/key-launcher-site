Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: MAY 20, 2026

1. Our Privacy Commitment

At Lousify Tech, we are committed to safeguarding your privacy when using the Key Launcher mobile application. Our core philosophy is built on absolute privacy and utility: all data processing is executed directly offline on your device, and we never collect, store, or transmit your personal information.

2. No Personal Data Collection

We do not maintain any backend servers to collect user inputs. Key Launcher:

  • Does NOT require email registration or user accounts.
  • Does NOT record, track, or save your T9 numeric search queries.
  • Does NOT aggregate your device's installed application list on external servers.
  • Does NOT use cookies, advertisement tracking SDKs, or third-party trackers designed to profile user behavior.

3. Android System Permissions

To operate correctly as a system replacement launcher, Key Launcher requires certain standard Android permissions. All data related to these permissions is handled temporarily and strictly offline:

  • Query All Packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Required to scan and display your installed applications for launching when you type T9 combinations.
  • Read Contacts (READ_CONTACTS) (Optional): Used only if you configure speed-dial T9 keys to place calls directly from your home screen.
  • Notification Listener Access (Optional): Required for the Quick Glance feature to aggregate and present pending notification counters in a single distraction-free widget.

4. Third-Party Services

Our application is distributed via the official Google Play Store. Google Play Store may automatically collect crash reports and anonymous statistics according to its standard privacy policies. You can consult the Google Play Services privacy policy on their official portal for details.

5. Policy Modifications

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to align with software updates. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page. Your continued use of the application following updates constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

6. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Key Launcher's offline security mechanisms, feel free to contact Lousify Tech via our official Reddit community channel at r/keylauncher.
